## Privacy Policy for Linkjob AI - Interview Notes

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Linkjob AI - Interview Notes, a Chrome extension published by Linkjob AI. It explains how the extension handles information when you create, save, copy, or delete an interview note.

This policy covers the Chrome extension only. The separate Linkjob AI website and desktop application have their own privacy policies.

## Information You Enter

The extension handles text that you choose to enter into the following fields:

* Company
* Role
* Interview question
* My answer
* Next steps

This information is used only to display, save, and copy your interview note.

The extension does not access the contents of websites you visit, your browsing history, microphone, camera, or screen.

## Local Storage and Retention

Your current note is automatically saved in the extension’s local browser storage on your device.

The extension does not upload your notes to Linkjob AI, send them to an AI model, or synchronize them between devices. No account is required, and the extension does not provide cloud storage or cloud backup.

Your saved note remains until you clear it, remove the extension, or the browser’s extension storage is deleted.

The extension does not provide a separate password or encryption layer for stored notes. Someone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to access locally stored information.

## Clipboard Access

When you click Copy notes, the extension writes your current note to your system clipboard as formatted plain text.

The extension uses the clipboardWrite permission for this action. It does not read your clipboard or request the clipboardRead permission.

Your device and operating system may provide clipboard history or clipboard synchronization. Those features are controlled by your device settings, not by this extension.

## Editing and Deleting Your Notes

You can edit your note at any time.

To delete the note saved by the extension, click Clear note and confirm the deletion.

Deleting a note from the extension does not delete copies you have pasted into documents, emails, or other applications. It also does not clear your system clipboard or clipboard history.

## Data Transmission, Sharing, and Analytics

The extension does not transmit your notes to Linkjob AI or third-party servers. It contains no analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or remotely executed code.

Linkjob AI does not receive, sell, or share your notes through this extension. Your notes are not used for advertising, profiling, AI model training, creditworthiness assessments, or lending decisions.

## Links to the Linkjob AI Website

The optional Visit Linkjob AI link opens https://www.senseiai.me/.

Your note is not included in that link or automatically sent to the website.

When you visit the website, its own privacy policy applies. The website may use cookies and process information separately from this extension.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use Compliance

The extension uses information only to provide its user-facing note-taking functionality.

Its handling of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the extension’s functionality or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised.

Relevant Chrome Web Store disclosures will also be updated when necessary.
